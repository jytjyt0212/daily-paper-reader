## 摘要
我们提出一种方法，用于从离散变量上的概率分布中恢复因果DAG的道德图，该方法使用全连接张量网络（FCTNs）以及核范数正则化的键修正。每个键矩阵被参数化为一个基线全1矩阵加上一个低秩修正 $C_{ij} = U_{ij}V_{ij}^\top$，而通过对因子施加变分Frobenius范数惩罚来实现的修正的核范数会将不必要的键驱动为零。我们证明，在忠实性、正性以及对局部张量架构的无隐式重路由假设下，\textbf{每一个}重建误差为零 $\varepsilon = 0$ 的最优FCTN，其有效图都恰好等于道德图。对于近似情形（$\varepsilon > 0$），我们利用条件互信息的Fannes-Audenaert连续性给出了显式恢复界，并推导出正则化参数 $\beta$ 的一个充分条件。有效图可直接从优化后的键矩阵中读出。

## Abstract
We present a method for recovering the moral graph of a causal DAG from a probability distribution over discrete variables, using fully connected tensor networks (FCTNs) with nuclear-norm-regularized bond corrections. Each bond matrix is parameterized as a baseline all-ones matrix plus a low-rank correction $C_{ij} = U_{ij}V_{ij}^\top$, and the nuclear norm of the correction implemented via the variational Frobenius norm penalty on the factors drives unnecessary bonds to zero. We prove that under faithfulness, positivity, and a no-implicit-rerouting assumption on the local tensor architecture, \textbf{every} optimal FCTN with zero reconstruction error $\varepsilon = 0$ has effective graph exactly equal to the moral graph. For the approximate regime ($\varepsilon > 0$), we provide explicit recovery bounds using the Fannes-Audenaert continuity of conditional mutual information, and derive a sufficient condition on the regularization parameter $β$. The effective graph is read directly from the optimized bond matrices.
